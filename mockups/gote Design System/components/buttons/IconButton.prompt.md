One-line: round icon-only button — the app's back/close control and on-photo chrome buttons.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="chevron-back" />            {/* back */}
<IconButton icon="close" />                   {/* close */}
<IconButton icon="close" variant="photo" />   {/* white, over a photo */}
```

Notes: `faint` (default) is a 40px circle on the sunken fill; `photo` is bare white; `brand` is solid green. Glyph names are Ionicons.
