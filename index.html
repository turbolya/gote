Learn the species you see

Quick flashcard rounds built from real nature photos — birds, plants, insects, fungi and more

See a organism and name what you’re looking at

Every card is a real observation, with five believable options drawn from genuine look-alikes — so you learn the difference, not just the answer.

Feel yourself getting sharper

A daily streak to keep, a line that climbs as you play, and your own accuracy for every species you’ve met.

Detailed analytics, species by species

See exactly which ones you’ve nailed and which keep tripping you up — every right and wrong answer, ranked by how well you know each.

Learn the local fauna and flora

Drop a pin and study the species most commonly seen there — drawn from every iNaturalist observer around that spot, not just your own records.

Screens show a sample account — your stats and species will be your own.

Play it your way — by name, by picture, speedrun, flash cards or a custom set — from a starter deck or your own iNaturalist observations.

Also on Apple Watch.

Play a quick round on your wrist, and keep your lifetime accuracy and streak right on your watch face.

On the horizon

Where gote is headed

A few planned features

Planned 01

Shareable custom decks

Build your own set — a local patch, a field trip, a class syllabus — and share it with anyone through a single link.

Planned 02

Spaced repetition

Smarter practice that resurfaces each species right before you’d forget it, so the names stick for good.

Planned 03

Adaptive sessions

Rounds that quietly lean into your weak spots and ease off on the ones you’ve already mastered.

For the perpetually curious.

The birders, the plant people, and anyone who’s ever crouched over something small and wondered what on earth it was.
