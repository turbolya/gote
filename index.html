A look inside

A calm, photo-first interface that keeps the species front and centre.

Pick a mode and play

Six ways to study the same photos, so recognition never gets stale.

🖼️

By name

See a photo, choose the right species from the options.

🔤

By picture

See a name, pick the matching photo from real look-alikes.

⚡

Speedrun

A photo flashes for a few seconds — then race the clock.

🃏

Flash cards

Flip to reveal the answer, then grade yourself.

📍

Nearby species

Drop a pin and learn what’s commonly seen there, from every observer around that spot.

⌚

On your wrist

A quick quiz and your streak on Apple Watch, synced from your phone.

Track your progress

The more you play, the more it learns what you already know.

📚

Your lexicon

A searchable list of every species you’ve seen, filtered by how well you know each.

📈

Statistics & streak

Per-species accuracy, a daily streak, and an accuracy chart that grows as you play.

🌿

Built for naturalists

Start with a sample set, or study your own iNaturalist observations. Every photo credits its photographer.

Turn your sightings into a game

Whether you’re a birder sharpening your IDs, a student learning local flora, or just curious about your backyard — gote turns recognition into a few fun minutes a day.
