Learn the species you see.

Quick flashcard rounds built from real nature photos — birds, plants, insects, fungi and more. Free, no ads, no account needed.

See a photo. Name what you’re looking at.

Every card is a real observation, with five believable options drawn from genuine look-alikes — so you learn the difference, not just the answer.

Feel yourself getting sharper.

A daily streak to keep, a line that climbs as you play, and your own accuracy for every species you’ve met.

A field guide that builds itself.

Everything you’ve seen gathers in one searchable place. Or drop a pin on the map and learn what actually lives there.

Play it your way — by name, by picture, speedrun, or flashcards, from a sample set or your own iNaturalist observations. There’s an Apple Watch app, too.

For the perpetually curious.

The birders, the plant people, and anyone who’s ever crouched over something small and wondered what on earth it was.
